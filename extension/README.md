# 🚀 MIS Volopay 1-Click Sync Bridge (Chrome Extension)

This extension enables **100% direct, one-click syncing** from your MIS Expenses Dashboard without ever needing to open the Terminal or click on `.command` files.

---

## 🛠️ 1-Minute One-Time Setup

1. Open **Google Chrome**.
2. In the URL bar, go to: `chrome://extensions/`
3. In the top right corner, toggle **Developer mode** to **ON**.
4. In the top left corner, click **Load unpacked**.
5. Select this exact folder:
   ```
   /Users/abhishekkumar/Desktop/MIS3 just expenses/extension
   ```
6. You will see **MIS Volopay 1-Click Sync Bridge** appear in your extension list.

---

## ⚡ How to Use
1. Have [https://iskconwhitefield.volopay.co.in](https://iskconwhitefield.volopay.co.in) logged in in Chrome.
2. Open your dashboard:
   👉 **https://mis-expenses-dashboard.zoom-attendance-live.workers.dev/**
3. Click the **"Sync data"** button.
4. That's it! The extension automatically captures the active Volopay session, pushes the fresh tokens to Cloudflare, updates Google Sheets, updates Cloudflare D1, and refreshes the dashboard in under 10 seconds.
